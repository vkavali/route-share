# SeatPal full-width landing plan

The visitor is deciding whether to find a shared seat or offer space on a trip already planned. The opening gives them a real route draft form before sign-in; it does not show invented availability or geocode their text. Exact pickup and destination remain an authenticated app choice.

Use a fluid page up to 1440px with roughly 4vw gutters: a raised, crisp search panel on the left and a large dimensional Hyderabad illustration on the right. On phones, both use the full useful width within 20px gutters. White, porcelain, charcoal and gray carry the page; blue is limited to focus and a small logo accent.

Remove every decorative page spine, progress track and track node from the DOM and scroll logic. Keep the five real capabilities as broad alternating sections: the heading and actionable feature explanation occupy one side; the existing honest example occupies the other. Icons accompany their feature titles rather than floating on a track. Rounded section edges and restrained shadows establish depth without turning every paragraph into a card.

Preserve search/offer intent validation and sign-in handoff, static-preview plain login behavior, expandable notes, example state controls, journey replay, role tabs, language and public FAQ/support/legal links. Empty/identical local routes show an error; static previews allow plain login without disposable labels. EN/TE, 16px inputs, 44px controls, reduced motion and no-JS app links are required.

The brand component is owned separately: include the approved stable seated car/bike crossfade partial; do not recreate its paths or stretch the seat.

This is an uncommitted local preview. The previous concept is preserved in output/design-snapshots/neutral-concept3-20261014. No deployment is implied.
