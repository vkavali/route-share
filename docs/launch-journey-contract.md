# Joined release journey contract

This contract covers a passenger joining a planned trip with a separately reviewed driver. It describes the current local product path; synthetic OTP and route providers prove application behavior only, not live identity, SMS, road, or payment services.

## User actions and guarantees

1. A new passenger or driver starts public phone signup, verifies the provider challenge, accepts the current terms, privacy notice, and adult attestation, then saves a name. Signup creates an individual member account, not a driver or staff approval. Before signup, travel data is empty. Invalid, expired, replayed, or provider-failed challenges do not create an authenticated account; retry starts or resumes the bounded challenge flow. A completed signup can be repeated to resume the same account.
2. Each participant submits identity evidence and consent through the private identity application, and an authorized reviewer reads the evidence and records a versioned decision. The driver separately submits driving and vehicle evidence for review. Until both participants have current identity reviews and the driver has current vehicle-matched approval, request/accept/start gates fail closed. Empty applications return `null`; stale versions, missing evidence, self-review, and unauthorized access return errors without granting approval. Review is a human evidence check, not government identity authentication or a safety guarantee.
3. The reviewed driver previews and publishes a trip with declared seat and baggage capacity and an upfront price. A passenger searches the requested route and baggage needs; a matching result carries the current quote snapshot and route evidence when available. A request stores that snapshot and baggage declaration. Acceptance rechecks eligibility, capacity, route windows, and the saved quote atomically before consuming a seat. A retry with stale state must refresh and cannot silently change the accepted amount. Missing route evidence makes a route-rate offer unavailable; incompatible baggage or full capacity also excludes a match. A legacy flat-price trip with no declared price may still match and be requested with an unknown (`null`) quote, which the app does not turn into an amount.
4. Either participant may cancel a pending or accepted booking with a reason while its trip is `published` or `started`, including after boarding. The booking becomes `cancelled`, its seat segment is released, and its cancellation actor/time are recorded. Accepted cancellations inside two hours of pickup receive a late marker; there is no cancellation fee. A repeated cancellation is idempotent. A segment already completed and a completed trip cannot be cancelled. The driver may cancel their own published or started trip with a reason; unresolved pending/accepted bookings are cancelled, completed segments remain completed, and the cancellation actor/time are recorded. Cancellation preserves the last agreed route timetable and does not reroute remaining passengers. Decline remains a driver action for pending requests only. Trying a new request requires a current match and current capacity. Cancellation does not itself settle, void, or refund a financial transfer.
5. The driver starts the published trip only while current eligibility holds. Passenger and driver may confirm that passenger's segment complete; the driver can complete the overall trip after accepted segments are resolved. Completed records survive application restart. Invalid transitions return conflict and preserve the last committed state.

## Financial boundary

An accepted quote is an immutable offer snapshot, not a charge or proof of a payment. Current route-rate quotes are local-preview behavior and encode zero platform fee and zero tax; production fees, taxes, driver net, and profitability policy are not configured. Current direct UPI, when explicitly enabled, is a manual handoff and participant acknowledgement: opening a UPI link does not prove settlement, and payment/refund status changes are not backed by a gateway. It defaults off. No gateway capture, signed webhook, payout, automatic refund, or settlement reconciliation is claimed. Do not describe this journey as a paid trip or as live payment readiness.

## Retry and recovery behavior

Mutating application actions use persisted versions and idempotency keys where defined. A matching retry returns the recorded result; key reuse with a different request conflicts. A lost response can be resolved by reading current bootstrap/state or the relevant application/booking, then retrying with the returned version. Reads of a new account return empty collections; protected reads and writes require authentication and current authorization. After a provider, routing, or storage error, read persisted state before retrying; do not assume the operation succeeded from a timeout or blindly resend an action that lacks an idempotency key.

This local contract does not claim real public SMS delivery, a hundred-user launch, or live payment/settlement readiness. Those require separately configured and verified providers, operational review, and end-to-end release evidence.
